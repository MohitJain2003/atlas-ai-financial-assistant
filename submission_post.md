🔷 *WHAT IS ATLAS?*

ATLAS is a 24/7 AI-powered Financial Analyst built inside Telegram. It eliminates context-switching between terminals, SEC filings, spreadsheets, news feeds, and messaging apps by bringing real-time market data, institutional research, and Google Workspace integration into a single natural conversation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🌟 *KEY HIGHLIGHTS & DIFFERENTIATORS*

🔑 *Google Workspace OAuth 2.0 Integration*
Connect your Google account via standard OAuth 2.0 to view Google Calendar events, search Gmail for market notes, and read Google Sheets natively.

⚡ *24/7 Uptime Keep-Alive Engine*
Equipped with an internal 4-minute self-ping heartbeat job that prevents Render server spin-down, ensuring 100% instant responses 24 hours a day.

📊 *Spreadsheet & Document Intelligence (Excel, PDF, DOCX, CSV)*
Upload an Excel portfolio (`.xlsx`), CSV, or PDF earnings report → ATLAS parses the tables, calculates cost basis, unrealized profit/loss %, and identifies top-performing holdings.

📈 *Real-Time Market Data & SEC EDGAR*
Fetches live quotes from Finnhub & Alpha Vantage, retrieves 10-K/10-Q SEC filings, Wall Street analyst consensus targets, and insider Form 4 transactions.

🎙️ *Voice Note Processing*
Send voice notes directly in Telegram — transcribed asynchronously via Groq Whisper (`whisper-large-v3`) and processed instantly by the AI engine.

🤖 *6-Provider Fallback Resiliency*
Zero downtime architecture backed by a 6-provider fallback chain (Gemini 2.0, Groq Llama 3.3 70B, OpenRouter, Mistral, SambaNova, Cerebras).

🔔 *Automated Daily Briefings & Custom Alerts*
Delivers personalized morning market briefings at your chosen time (e.g. 8:00 AM) and monitors custom price alerts in the background.

🛠️ *TECHNOLOGY STACK*

• *Backend:* Python 3.12, FastAPI, Uvicorn
• *Bot Framework:* python-telegram-bot (v21)
• *Primary AI Engine:* Gemini 2.0 Flash & Groq Llama 3.3 70B
• *Voice AI:* Groq Whisper Large v3
• *Market APIs:* Finnhub REST API, Alpha Vantage, SEC EDGAR
• *Google Integration:* Google Calendar API, Gmail API, Google Sheets API (OAuth 2.0)
• *Database & Scheduler:* Async SQLAlchemy (SQLite/PostgreSQL), APScheduler
• *Deployment:* Render Cloud with 24/7 Keep-Alive Heartbeat
